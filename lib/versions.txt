Some object needed editing:
- libcd/cdrom.o: reordered weak .bss variables
- libpad/waitrc2.o: reordered weak .bss variables
- libapi/free3.o: reordered weak .bss variables

In the original archives these variables are linker-placed (XBSS) records,
and the conversion to ELF laid them out in record order. PSYLINK orders them
by its symbol-table hash instead, (length + sum of the name's bytes) mod 512,
ascending, a later-seen name first on a tie: linking the original CDROM.obj,
WAITRC2.obj and FREE3.obj with PSYLINK 4.4, 4.5 and 4.6 gives exactly the
edited order. tools/patches/README.md has the details.

One object is not a conversion:
- libsn/snmain.o is assembled by the build from libsn/snmain.s, a reconstruction of
  libsn's SNMAIN.obj. The original holds psylink expressions - a section's start, end,
  size, a count - that ELF relocations cannot, so the converter cannot make it. Its
  instructions, data and symbols are the original's, its text and data byte for byte
  and every relocation where the original has a patch; each expression is a symbol the
  image's link script defines (__SN_*, listed in the source).
